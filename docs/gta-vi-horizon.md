# The GTA VI horizon

Grand Theft Auto VI is the project's most ambitious **long-term reference target**: a modern, demanding Xbox Series game that would stress every major compatibility boundary.

## What it would take
- **Application entry:** understand whether the relevant executable and launch model can be accommodated.
- **Runtime coverage:** support required services with correct semantics rather than dummy responses.
- **Graphics fidelity:** investigate complex rendering, shader translation, synchronization, and resource management.
- **Performance:** measure real workloads before making frame-rate projections.
- **Stability:** validate input, audio, persistence, threading, and long play sessions.

## Research before claims
No GTA VI executable, game package, or asset is hosted here. The project has not established GTA VI compatibility. The target is intended to guide engineering priorities; it is not a release commitment or an invitation to upload proprietary material.

A future demonstration must identify the precise version and hardware environment, provide an independently reproducible method, and distinguish menus, rendering, and genuine gameplay.

## Why aim high?
Because a compatibility architecture capable of supporting complex modern titles would represent much more than a single-game hack. The purpose of this research is to investigate that possibility step by step.
